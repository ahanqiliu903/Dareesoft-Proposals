# Dareesoft U.S. Expansion — Technical Fellow Projects

Built as part of the **NYU AI Innovation Accelerator (AIIA) Summer 2026** program, as a Technical Fellow to [Dareesoft](https://www.dareesoft.com), a Series B Korean AI road infrastructure startup expanding into the U.S. East Coast market.

Dareesoft's core product, RiaaS (Road Information as a Service), uses AI-powered dashcam devices to detect road hazards in real time and transmit data to city monitoring dashboards. They are currently running a pilot with the NYC DOT.

---

## Projects

### 1. Municipality Targeting and Outreach Tracker
A front-end tool to help the Dareesoft team systematically identify and track U.S. city outreach. Includes filtering by road budget, flagging displaced RoadBotics customers, and tracking RFP deadlines.
